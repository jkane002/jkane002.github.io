About

I’m a fourth year Computer Science major at UC Riverside graduating in June 2019.

My focus is in computer graphics where I aspire to be in the entertainment industry as a graphics programmer, software developer, production engineer, or creative technologist.

The goal is being a member of the animation, visual effects, simulation, virtual reality, or rendering community where I bridge the gap between computer science and digital art.

Outside of tech, I love playing soccer and basketball (#LAbron), gymming, drinking boba, eating Ethiopian food, discovering new Spotify artists, and attending concerts.

Catch me at #SIGGRAPH2019!
